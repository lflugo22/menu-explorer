## Configuration

> Configure measurement parameters, intervals, outputs, and cleaning for the analyzer.

### name
> Changes the name of the analyzer (up to 16 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, or punctuation).

### parameter
> Selects the measured parameter shown on the display.
- nh4_n: NH4-N
- nh4: NH4

### unit
> Selects the measurement unit shown on the display.
- mg_l: mg/L
- ppb: ppb

### measurement_range
> Selects the applicable measurement range; changing the range requires installing the corresponding standard solutions and electrolyte.
- range_0_02_5: 0.02 - 5 mg/L
- range_0_05_20: 0.05 - 20 mg/L
- range_1_100: 1 - 100 mg/L
- range_10_1000: 10 - 1000 mg/L

### output_mode
> Sets the values shown on the analog outputs when the analyzer is in maintenance mode.
- hold: Hold — analog outputs retain the latest measured value
- active: Active — analog outputs continue to show the measured parameter
- transfer: Transfer — sets analog outputs to the configured Transfer value

### measurement_interval
> Selects the measurement interval; the recommended default of 10 minutes keeps the maintenance interval at half a year.
- interval_5: 5 minutes
- interval_10: 10 minutes
- interval_15: 15 minutes
- interval_20: 20 minutes
- interval_30: 30 minutes

### sampling
> Sets the channel for the sample filtration system to On or Off.

### cleaning
> Shows the start time of the next cleaning and configures automatic cleaning settings.

#### cleaning_start_time
> Sets the start time for the automatic cleaning cycle.

#### cleaning_interval
> Sets the automatic cleaning interval; the recommended default of 48 hours keeps the maintenance interval at half a year.
- off: Off
- hours_6: 6 hours
- hours_12: 12 hours
- hours_24: 24 hours
- hours_48: 48 hours

#### extended_cleaning
> Sets the cleaning procedure of the overflow vessel and sample drain tubing to On or Off; manufacturer recommends Off when a Nitratax sc or NT3100sc sensor is the last device in a cascaded installation.

### seasonal_heating
> Selects the starting and ending month for the operating time of the heated drain tubing.

### sample_flow_warning
> Sets the warning level for sample flow (400–1400 mL/h; default 600 mL/h); a warning appears when sample flow decreases below this threshold.

### maintenance_reminder
> Sets the number of days before maintenance is due when a reminder is shown on the display.
- day_1: 1 day
- days_3: 3 days
- days_7: 7 days
- days_14: 14 days
- days_21: 21 days
- days_28: 28 days

### out_of_range_warning
> Sets the warning for low and high out-of-range measurement values to On or Off.

### reset_to_defaults
> Sets all configuration settings to factory defaults; measurement range and counters are not reset.

### channel_1
> Settings specific to Channel 1 on a 2-channel analyzer.

#### ch1_location
> Changes the name of the Channel 1 location (up to 16 characters).

#### ch1_parameter
> Selects the measured parameter shown for Channel 1.
- ch1_nh4_n: NH4-N
- ch1_nh4: NH4

#### ch1_number_of_measurements
> Sets the number of measurements for Channel 1.

#### ch1_discard_measurements
> Sets the number of measurements discarded after a channel change and before actual measurements start for Channel 1.

#### ch1_sampling_configuration
> Sets Channel 1 to internal or external filtration system.
- ch1_int: Channel 1 - Internal sampling
- ch1_ext: Channel 1 - External sampling

### channel_2
> Settings specific to Channel 2 on a 2-channel analyzer.

#### ch2_location
> Changes the name of the Channel 2 location (up to 16 characters).

#### ch2_parameter
> Selects the measured parameter shown for Channel 2.
- ch2_nh4_n: NH4-N
- ch2_nh4: NH4

#### ch2_number_of_measurements
> Sets the number of measurements for Channel 2.

#### ch2_discard_measurements
> Sets the number of measurements discarded after a channel change and before actual measurements start for Channel 2.

## Calibration

> Configure and start calibration cycles using two standards to calculate offset and slope.

### start_calibration_now
> Starts a calibration cycle immediately.

### next_calibration
> Shows the date and time when the next scheduled calibration will start.

### automatic_calibration
> Configures automatic calibration settings.

#### auto_cal_start_time
> Sets the time at which the automatic calibration cycle starts.

#### auto_cal_interval
> Sets the interval for automatic calibration; the recommended default of 48 hours keeps the maintenance interval at half a year.
- off: Off
- hours_6: 6 hours
- hours_12: 12 hours
- hours_24: 24 hours
- hours_48: 48 hours

### last_calibration_value
> Shows the date, time, status, mV zero, mV standard 1 and 2, mV slope, and mV offset of the most recent calibration.

### grab_sample
> Starts the automatic grab sample procedure (requires the optional grab sample accessory).

#### start_grab_sample
> Fills a grab sample bottle with sample and records the measurement.

#### last_grab_sample
> Shows the date, time, and value of the most recent grab sample.

### validation
> Starts the automatic validation procedure (requires the optional validation accessory).

#### start_validation
> Pre-pumps, flushes, and measures the installed standard solution three times.

#### last_validation
> Shows the date, time, and value of the most recent validation.

### factor
> Sets the slope factor to adjust the standard calibration (range: 0.500–2.000; default: 1.000).

### offset
> Sets the offset value to adjust the standard calibration (range: –5.00 to 5.00 mg/L; default: 0.00).

### cal_reset_to_defaults
> Sets all calibration settings to factory defaults.

### channel_1_correction
> Correction settings for Channel 1 on a 2-channel analyzer.

#### ch1_location_name
> Shows the name of Channel 1 location.

#### ch1_factor
> Sets the slope factor for Channel 1 (range: 0.500–2.000; default: 1.000).

#### ch1_offset
> Sets the offset value for Channel 1 (range: –5.00 to 5.00 mg/L; default: 0.00).

### channel_2_correction
> Correction settings for Channel 2 on a 2-channel analyzer.

#### ch2_location_name
> Shows the name of Channel 2 location.

#### ch2_factor
> Sets the slope factor for Channel 2 (range: 0.500–2.000; default: 1.000).

#### ch2_offset
> Sets the offset value for Channel 2 (range: –5.00 to 5.00 mg/L; default: 0.00).

## Maintenance

> Access maintenance workflows, replacement procedures, cleaning, and shutdown options.

### current_status
> Shows the current operating status of the analyzer.

### remaining_time
> Shows the time remaining in the current process.

### start_maintenance_mode
> Toggles between maintenance mode and operational mode.

### six_month_maintenance
> Starts the 6-month maintenance procedure (guided or non-guided) consolidating all necessary tasks; after completion all counters are automatically reset to zero.

#### start_guided
> Starts the 6-month maintenance with step-by-step on-screen instructions.

#### start_non_guided
> Starts the 6-month maintenance without on-screen instructions.

### cleaning
> Cleaning procedures for the analyzer and sample system.

#### automatic_cleaning
> Starts an automatic cleaning procedure (approximately 30 minutes); flushes all tubing and parts with cleaning solution.

#### filter_module_cleaning
> Provides guidance for cleaning the filter modules of FX610 and FX620.

#### clean_sample_tubing
> Provides guidance for manually cleaning the sample tubing.

### prepumping
> Starts prepumping options to prime tubing with the selected fluid.
- prepump_all: Prepump all
- reagent: Reagent
- standard_solutions_1_2: Standard solutions 1 & 2
- cleaning_solution: Cleaning solution
- sample_pump_tubing: Sample pump tubing
- sample_preparation: Sample

### replacement
> Individual maintenance replacement tasks with guided or non-guided instructions; counter resets to zero after each completed workflow.

#### air_filter_pads
> Replace the two air filter pads on the right side of the analyzer.

#### reagent
> Replace the reagent bottle.

#### standard_solutions_1_2
> Replace standard solution 1 and standard solution 2 bottles.

#### cleaning_solution_replacement
> Replace the cleaning solution bottle.

#### electrolyte_membrane
> Replace the electrolyte and membrane cap of the gas-sensitive electrode.

#### sample_pump_tubing
> Replace the peristaltic sample pump tubing (optional; only for analyzers with internal filtration pump).

#### filter_module
> Replace the filter module (refer to FX610/FX620 user manual).

#### piston_pump_head
> Replace the piston pump head (recommended every 12 months).

#### electrode
> Replace the gas-sensitive electrode unit.

### air_tightness_test
> Starts an air tightness test to check for leaks in the piston pump system.

### reset_errors
> Clears all active errors from the analyzer.

### shutdown
> Options for taking the analyzer out of operation.

#### shutdown_short
> Puts the analyzer in shutdown mode for a short period (up to 2 days); no special draining measures required.

#### extended_shutdown
> Prepares the analyzer for extended shutdown or relocation by draining all chemicals and rinsing tubing with distilled water.

### factory_service
> Access factory service menu (password required).

## Diagnostics

> View analyzer information, signal states, and maintenance counters.

### device_information
> Shows device name, given name, location(s), measurement range, serial number, part number, and software/hardware versions.

### signals
> Shows all functional condition signals of the analyzer.

### counters
> Shows the number of days remaining until each maintenance task is due; counters reset when guided or non-guided maintenance workflows are completed.
